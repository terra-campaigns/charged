## Active Rulings

- **2026-10-18 — Oldtech Naming Protocol:** When proposing or creating names for oldtech artefacts, facilities, machines, and resurrected fauna, strictly apply Gene Wolfe's four-phase protocol:
  1. *Functional & Physical Deconstruction*: Strip the technology down to its raw geometry, material behaviour, and secondary degraded utility.
  2. *Lexical Substitution / Pseudotranslation*: Never invent science-fiction techno-jargon. Substitute authentic archaic military, civic, heraldic, liturgical, or palaeontological terrestrial lexemes that describe the object's current social function, or use phonetically drifted historical surnames.
  3. *Sensory Estrangement (Ostranenie)*: Frame descriptions through raw sensory impressions and folklore utility, burying any engineering clue inside a casual parenthetical aside.
  4. *Delayed Taxonomic Traps*: When introducing an entity with a familiar historical label (e.g. *destrier*, *courier chough*), withhold its mechanical or anatomical divergence for subsequent encounters. - **2026-10-18 — Prose Cadence & Wolfean Register:** In-setting campaign prose, session chronicles, and narrative descriptions should operationalise Gene Wolfe's cadence:
  - *Anchor-and-Veil Structure*: Pair hyper-specific, grounded sensory anchors (corrosion, fractured glass, soiled silk, river fog) with cool, aristocratic composure, technical parenthetical clues, and detached philosophical aphorisms rationalising questionable deeds.
  - *80/20 Clarity-to-Obscurity Ratio*: Maintain syntactic transparency and declarative economy across 80% of the sentence. Limit obscure or archaic terms to 20% (one or two precise nouns per sentence) to prevent purple parody.
  - *Clinical Detachment*: Treat grotesque body horror, executions, and strange technologies with surgical, professional nonchalance rather than melodramatic flourish.
