# Session Prep (UVG+24XX)

A structured, interactive procedure to prepare the next session of the Charged campaign. The process walks the GM step-by-step through 8 distinct preparation phases, integrating canon lore, character threads, and random tables into a table-ready briefing.

## Guiding Principles

- **Iterative and Conversational**: Do not dump all steps in a single monolithic response. Guide the GM through each step one at a time, presenting options, incorporating their steer, and refining before advancing to the next step.
- **Canon Grounding**: Draw consistently from Tier 1 published notes (`campaigns/`, `directory/`, `trading/`), Tier 2 active rulings (`.agents/context/decisions.md`), and Tier 3 reference digests in `_digests/` (`uvg_2e_core.md`, `metamorphica_revised.md`, etc.).
- **Wolfean Register**: Emphasise the anchor-and-veil aesthetic — pairing concrete sensory anchors (grit, static, fractured porcelain, ozone) with detached philosophical aphorisms and clinical composure.
- **Oldtech Naming Protocol**: When introducing ancient machinery or relics, strictly follow Gene Wolfe's four-phase naming protocol (deconstruction, archaic lexical substitution, sensory estrangement, and delayed taxonomic traps).

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 0 — NOW: Define Current Date & Location

- Establish the current campaign date based on the latest published session log front matter (`nav_order` in `campaigns/UVG24XX/ep_XXX.md`).
- Confirm the current physical location of the caravan (station, campsite, district, or landmark).
- Note the ambient conditions, caravan morale, and any immediate time-sensitive countdowns or resting requirements.
- Prompt the GM to confirm or advance the calendar date and verify the campsite.

### Step 1 — PCS: Review Characters & Elevate Lore (weave-threads)

- Review the roster of player characters attending the upcoming session.
- Check their character notes in `directory/Atles/` and past session appearances.
- Apply `weave-threads` logic:
  - Identify open debt contracts, financier pressures, or guild obligations.
  - Elevate physical conditions, traumas, or mutations (e.g. Pilixa's paraplegia).
  - Surface personal agendas, background secrets, and active trade good interests intersecting the caravan's current location.
- Present tailored narrative hooks and stakes for each attending PC to engage them directly.

### Step 2 — IMR: In-Media-Res First Scene (weave-threads)

- Design a punchy, atmospheric opening scene that thrusts the party immediately into the middle of action, tension, or dilemma.
- Elevate recently documented or discovered lore from recent session logs and local directory entries rather than starting in passive downtime.
- Craft the scene using sensory anchors (smells, tactile grit, eerie acoustics) and immediate stakes demanding a decision or reaction from the players.

### Step 3 — MIS: Weather & Misfortune Table

- Construct a tailored table of weather, regional hazards, and travel misfortunes specific to the current location.
- Draw from the UVG location chapter (climate, atmospheric peculiarities, toxic dusts, electrostatic discharges) and the d20 *Luck of the Road* table in `uvg_2e_core.md`.
- Ensure each entry has concrete consequences suited to 24XX mechanics: ability hindrances, gear damage, supply loss, temporal delays, or escalating hazards.

### Step 4 — ENC: d6 Encounters Table (Electric Bastionland Framework & Metamorphica)

- Build a focused d6 encounter table employing Chris McDowall's Small Tables framework.
- **Interactive Guidance Rule**: Never generate the entire table at once. Guide the GM interactively tier-by-tier:
  - **1: Rare** — an exceptional oddity, apex predator, reality-distorting anomaly, or singular wandering eccentric. Present 2–3 evocative seeds from UVG or *After the Fall*; let the GM select or refine before advancing.
  - **2–3: Uncommon** — distinct regional factions or rival groups. Inquire whether the GM wants two contrasting factions or two distinct aspects/squads of a single faction.
  - **4–6: Common (Forward Pulls & Trade Skews)** — environmental baseline and local life. Preferentially structure these as active **propulsive levers** pulling the caravan toward further trail stations (e.g. trade distress sales, forward delivery contracts, or rival haulers competing on an identified price skew).
- Incorporate encounter concepts from the UVG location pages and the *After The Fall* post-apocalyptic mutants chapter in *The Metamorphica (Revised)* (pp. 157–180).
- **Dynamic Weave-Threads Integration**: If faction motivations or market reasons for movement are underspecified, offer to run `weave-threads` midway through prep to unearth latent lore or economic anomalies before locking in the encounters.
- Format each entry with two clear components:
  1. **Who/What is it?** (Sensory appearance, strange physical or biological trait).
  2. **What are they doing right now?** (Current activity, urgent motivation, or active predicament).

### Step 5 — ALR: Artefact, Landmarks & Routes (1-2-3)

Prepare a tripartite discovery dossier for the location:

1. **1 Artefact**: An ancient relic, machine component, or oldtech curiosity available through salvage, trade, or discovery. Combine the UVG relic tables with *The Metamorphica (Revised)* *Ficto-Technica* generator (pp. 181–220): prefix, core mechanism, output effect, and operational glitch. Apply the Tier 2 Gene Wolfe four-phase naming protocol.
2. **2 Landmarks**: Two evocative local landmarks, architectural ruins, or natural curiosities drawn from the UVG location text or local directory to serve as navigational beacons or exploration targets.
3. **3 Routes & In-Location Movement Helper**:
   - **In-Location Movement Helper**: A generic, modular environmental helper for navigating within the campsite or settlement during local weather/hazards (visibility limits, weather mechanics, grounding checks), strictly excluding encounters so they remain managed by the encounter table.
   - **3 Outward Routes**: Three distinct routes or travel corridors leading out of the location (from the UVG book and map), specifying travel time (in days or weeks), terrain hazards, supply demands, and trade potential.

### Step 6 — INF: d6 Table of Information & Rumours

- Construct a d6 table of actionable clues, overheard rumours, market tips, and warnings to feed to the players across the session.
- Weave together:
  - Recent campaign developments and faction schemes.
  - Trade goods price skews and arbitrage intelligence.
  - Unexplored wilderness hazards or mythic destination hints.
  - Half-truths, eerie prophecies, and merchant exaggerations.

### Step 7 — MAP: Map Updates & Cartography

- Guide the GM through verifying and updating the campaign maps:
  - Check the caravan marker and route on the [Ultraviolet Grasslands Owlbear Rodeo map](https://www.owlbear.rodeo/room/S4p4WuPKKULl/charged).
  - Verify directory backlinks and location links between the current campsite, visited landmarks, and prospective routes.
  - Create or update any required stub notes in `directory/` for newly established landmarks or waypoints.

## Session Prep Artifact

Once all steps are finalised with the GM, compile the results into a clean markdown prep artifact or note (e.g. `campaigns/UVG24XX/prep_ep_XXX.md`) formatted for quick reference behind the screen during play.
