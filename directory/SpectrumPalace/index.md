A drum-shaped redoubt of dull metal and rivets, thirty metres across and a hundred in circuit, nestled in a shallow saddle between unremarkable hills.

Every night, full-spectrum localized aurorae wash across the heavens above the palace, casting dazzling iridescent light through the surrounding Refracting Trees.
Within its subterranean crystal vaults throbs the Memorium, the immortal telepathic hive-engine and ancestral home of the [Spectrum Satraps](SpectrumSatraps.md).
