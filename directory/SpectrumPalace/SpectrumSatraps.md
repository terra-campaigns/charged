The 360 original flaming telepaths who dwell as creatures of coherent light within the aurorae and crystal lattices of the [Spectrum Palace](index.md).

Unclad, a Satrap burns like a pocket star, blinding all who look upon them before evaporating into thin ether within minutes.
To walk the physical steppe, they encase themselves in synthetic skin-suits, scales of steel-glass, or heavy golem machinery.
Should a bodily vessel be destroyed, their core consciousness is reconstructed from the crystal matrices of the Memorium for a fee of several thousand sovereigns.

## The Memorium & The Ecstatics

The heart of Satrapy existence is the Memorium: a resonating psychic machine-community that fuses the personalities of the Satraps with the minds of their devoted worshippers.
These disciples, known as Ecstatics, willingly inhale crystal fungus spores.
Over years of euphoric prayer, the spores slowly consume their mortal brains, absorbing their memories and emotions directly into the communal mind.

The Satraps are governed by The Five: an executive gestalt of five Satraps neurally fused by council-symbionts into a singular governing mind.
The Five are re-selected by lot every five months by an ancient, limbless archaic suspended within the Hall of Choice.

## Light Magic & Technics

The Satraps preserve the force-shaping disciplines of the Long-Long-Ago.
They wield radiant laser rifles, weave blinding optical illusions, and extrude solid sheets of hard light capable of acting as bridges, walls, or deathly razor-fences.
Within their synthetic suits they harbor bio-engineered endosymbionts—many-fingered monkey-lizards, optic slugs, and venomous rabbit-snakes trained for covert tasks.

## Grassland Presence & Trade Duopoly

Together with their eternal rivals, the [Porcelain Princes](../PorcelainCitadel/PorcelainPrinces.md), the Satraps command an ironclad trade duopoly controlling the flow of goods across the Ultraviolet Grasslands.
While the Princes govern the eastern approach near the [Porcelain Citadel](../PorcelainCitadel/index.md), the Satraps rule the west from the [Spectrum Palace](index.md).

Along the central trail, they project influence through fortified outposts and extra-territorial embassies:

- **Final Embassy of the Satraps**: Maintained at [The Last Serai](../LastSerai/index.md). It houses their resident seer (a swollen-headed oracle floating in nutrient oils whose prophecies are notoriously, unfailingly inverted) and fixers/foremen who broker [wetwork contracts for standard currency](../../campaigns/UVG24XX/ep_011.md).
- **Full Spectrum Embassy**: Located further along the trail near [Fallen Umber](../FallenUmber/index.md).
- **The Spectrum Lodge**: A rest-stop and mission compound situated near the Near Moon.


## Loose ends at the Last Serai

The Final Embassy's foreman, contracted by the Atles caravan against [Iron Pot 6-body](../LastSerai/IronPot6-body.md), was found dead by Iron Pot.
Who killed him is unknown.
His Satrap suit was left behind when Iron Pot died, and the Satraps will recover it, as [it is valuable and can be mended](../../campaigns/UVG24XX/ep_012.md).
