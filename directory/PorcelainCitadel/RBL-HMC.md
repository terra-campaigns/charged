Expatriate guild-settlement of artisans, glaze-cookers, and scrap-dealers dwelling in the shadow of the [Porcelain Citadel](index.md).

They occupy the half-dugout subterranean quarter known as the Houses of Many Colours.
The tint and lustre of the glazed ceramic tiles cladding each roof and lintel advertise the owner's distant city and trade-house of origin in the Rainbowlands.

### Social Station & Trade

The community lives in precarious tolerance beneath the high balconies of the [Porcelain Princes](../../directory/LastSerai/Angel22-unity.md).
While the princes remain cloistered within their kiln-white towers, the Rainbowlanders process the base matter of the trail: firing porous pots, refining alchemical lubricants, and buying dryland chitin caps from passing prospectors.
They maintain the lower market where outland caravans resupply before pushing west across the dust.

Caravaneers seeking illicit trail knowledge or unpermitted salvage often deal through their tea-cellars and glaze workshops.
It was here that [Tam](../../directory/Atles/TamHR.md) bought intelligence regarding the forgotten *Qasso* metal mine in the Locust Spires ([ep_008](../../campaigns/UVG24XX/ep_008.md)).
