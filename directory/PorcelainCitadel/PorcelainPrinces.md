Not-quite-liches of the [Porcelain Citadel](index.md) who have cheated mortal rot by dividing their living sentience across distributed physical hulls.

They wear masks of polished kaolin and robes of stiff, embroidered silk.
Their cognitive continuity is maintained through real-time glandular psyche-to-psyche links, binding multiple bodies into a single aristocratic personality.
Should a single hull perish, the surviving bodies mourn with detached theatricality and purchase a replacement shell from the lower body-markets.

## The Polybody Network

A Prince is rarely a single creature.
They manifest as trios, dyads, quintets, or decadent swarms such as [Angel 22-unity](../LastSerai/Angel22-unity.md) in [The Last Serai](../LastSerai/index.md) and [Iron Pot 6-body](../LastSerai/IronPot6-body.md).
Because their bio-glandular links possess limited range, the constituent hulls of a Prince must remain in close physical proximity to prevent personality drift or the catastrophic insurrection of a rogue limb-cluster.

Inside the Citadel's high mosaicked villas, backups and juvenile shells sleep in nutrient vats tended by mute vivimancers.
Failed hulls whose cognitive resonance collapsed are discarded into the [Broken Line](index.md) or repurposed as domestic chassis.

## Trade & Governance

The Princes maintain a fierce conservative grip over the eastern caravan routes.
Together with their western counterparts, the [Spectrum Satraps](../SpectrumPalace/SpectrumSatraps.md), they enforce an ironclad trade duopoly over goods moving between the [Violet City](../VioletCity/index.md) and the distant Black City.

They finance expeditions and outland caravans through usurious debt contracts.
Their true hunger is not for coin, but for novel neural implants, biomechanical scrap, rare [alchemical lubricants](../../trading/TG01_Alchemical_lubricants.md), and fresh biological tithes to sustain their vat-laboratories.
