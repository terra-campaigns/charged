An esoteric biomantic death cult originating as a radical heresy within the ranks of the [Porcelain Princes](PorcelainPrinces.md).

While orthodox Porcelain Princes seek permanent bodily preservation across stable, multi-bodied polybody networks, the Ascendant Church views the biological vessel as inherently impure, combustible, and strictly disposable.
To the Church, mortal hulls are not shrines to be immortalised, but transient organic fuel-pods—crucibles to be burned through in pursuit of ecstatic psychic ascension and communion with the ancient dead.

## Creed of the Disposable Vessel

The central sacrament of the Church involves the systematic acquisition and consumption of "single-use bodies".
Through invasive psychic-glandular grafting, a cultist links into these disposable hulls for dangerous ritual channelings, lethal sensory experiments, or occult communion.

Once the physical vitality of the host body is depleted or burned out by metaphysical strain, the shell is ritually discarded, and the cultist's consciousness retreats into their primary kaolin core.

## The Bone Kaolin Remnant

In the modern era, the Church is largely decayed and whispered about in disgust by the conservative High Houses of the [Porcelain Citadel](index.md).

Its most notable surviving adherent is **[Bone Kaolin](BoneKaolin.md)**, a decayed 2-body Porcelain Prince who continues to wander the citadel's lower quarters in search of fresh biological hulls.
During their stay in the citadel ([ep_008](../../campaigns/UVG24XX/ep_008.md)), Bone Kaolin bestowed two miniature porcelain masks upon [Rise Ghost](../Atles/RiseGhost.md), formally claiming the travelling performer as their third body.
