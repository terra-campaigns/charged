A violet dodo with newly implanted solar-powered wings that let it fly.
Originally Vangogh, Tam's affinity-bonded pet, it was renamed Deep Purple by [Tam](TamHR.md) in a grassland-driven hallucination ([ep_010](../../campaigns/UVG24XX/ep_010.md)).

It was promised a Doda mate in exchange for flying past a crawler outside the Last Serai.
When nobody went looking for her, it grew angry and attacked Tam, leaving a limp ([ep_012](../../campaigns/UVG24XX/ep_012.md)).

This is [Tam](TamHR.md)'s aggressively upgraded Dodo, Vangogh, renamed Deep Purple when it received its solar-powered wings.
