A violet dodo with newly implanted solar-powered wings that let it fly.
Originally Vangogh, Tam's affinity-bonded pet, it was renamed Deep Purple by [Tam](TamHR.md) during a [grassland-driven hallucination](../../campaigns/UVG24XX/ep_010.md).

It was promised a Doda mate in exchange for flying past a crawler outside the Last Serai.
When nobody went looking for her, it grew angry and [attacked Tam, leaving a limp](../../campaigns/UVG24XX/ep_012.md).
