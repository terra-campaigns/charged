# Campaign Log

*This is an append-only narrative log of the UVG journey.*

## Session 1: The Argent Sloughing (24 April 3025)

The caravan prepares for departure in the Yard of the Seven Cisterns on the western rim of the [Violet City](../../directory/VioletCity/index.md).

While cinching a snapped pack harness with a bone knife, Oglianza accidentally scores their inner forearm.
Instead of blood, the outer *corium* reveals an **argent pellicle** that weeps dense quick-iron (*hydrargyrum*), pooling into the weighted form of a carnifex’s falchion.
Realising the blade is an extension of their own factitious anatomy, Oglianza wills the metal back into the arm.

Through supreme mental discipline (Thought d10 roll: 10), the quick-iron re-absorbs seamlessly, knitting the tallow-like skin shut without blemish or tremor.
Obscured behind the lead mule, Oglianza defuses the suspicious arrival of an Academic bailiff and two city watchmen, turning the inspection into a routine bureaucratic clearance.
