## The canvassed hall

[Iacum](../../directory/Atles/Iacum.md) arrives with the last caravan, as the electrostatic porcelain storm leans on [The Last Serai](../../directory/LastSerai/index.md).
The dust smells of fish.
It is a smell he has met before.

He finds [Tam](../../directory/Atles/TamHR.md), [Settler En-Perla](../../directory/Atles/Settler.md) and [Kur Barista](../../directory/Atles/Barista.md) in one of the common halls.
Doors and windows are hung with heavy canvas against the dust.

[Iron Pot](../../directory/LastSerai/IronPot6-body.md) comes in out of the storm, wearing the Satrap suit of the foreman.
He is bloodied, and his leg is broken.
"You unfixed me!" he says.

The foreman's fate remains unclear.
Iron Pot says only that he found him dead.

## The collapsed tent

Iacum and Settler persuade Iron Pot to come to Iacum's tent for a steppe recovery ritual.
Beneath the ritual lies a plan to kill him, though nobody agrees on the details.
The attempt is confused, and the tent comes down on everyone.

Iron Pot dies in the wreckage.
The canvas, the poles and the porcelain dust settle slowly afterwards.

While this happens, Deep Purple, Tam's violent dodo, grows angry that nobody is looking for the [promised Doda mate](ep_010.md).
It attacks Tam.
Tam is left with a limp.

Settler takes Iron Pot's mask and keeps it.
The armour is left behind.
The [Spectrum Satraps](../../directory/SpectrumPalace/SpectrumSatraps.md) will recover it in time, since it is valuable and can be mended.

## A week of dust and parties

A week follows.
The party researches the market, trades, and carouses heavily.
Kur keeps every receipt, to be repaid by [Angel 22-unity](../../directory/LastSerai/Angel22-unity.md) later.

They finally learn that the [Pit of Mould](../../directory/FoldedBelow/PitOfMould.md) lies three days away.
The fate of the Near Moon draws closer with every party.
At one of them, lightning strikes Kur, and his bones are weaker for it.

After the last party, the group talks about their pasts.
Iacum wants to know how this storm connects to his own history.
The link between Kur's single metal vertebra and his aged clone is severed through the lightning that night.
Tam sees that much of what has happened was prophesied by her own clone.

Tam expects the next prophecy to concern blood raining beneath a bloody moon.
She does not know whether this is the Near Moon.

## The Escaped Biochimera

As the porcelain storm phases out, everyone comes through it with a bad cough.
The dust seems to have settled in the lungs.

Something like a giant centipede climbs out of a window of the [Ignored Tower](../../directory/LastSerai/IgnoredTower.md).
It descends the outside of the tower, slowly.
At the bottom, it burrows into the ground.
The event becomes known as the Escaped Biochimera.

Everyone in the Serai is used to ignoring the tower.
They ignore this too.

Kur brings his receipts to Angel 22-unity, almost €1,500 spent on the death of Iron Pot.
Angel reimburses him, with 50% more, as agreed.
The receipts, at least, are in order.
