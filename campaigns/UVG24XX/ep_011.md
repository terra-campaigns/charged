## A rendezvous at the Serai

[Settler En-Perla](../../directory/Atles/Settler.md) and [Kalis Decano](../../directory/Atles/Kalis.md) arrive at [The Last Serai](../../directory/LastSerai/index.md), having previously agreed to meet here.
Kalis, an independent travelling entertainer fascinated by paintings, arrives with his lucky, unkillable warrior companion.
Settler, an exiled Bluelander with a proud moustache, busies himself enforcing political apprehension.

They link up with [Kur Barista](../../directory/Atles/Barista.md) and [Pilixa](../../directory/Atles/Pilixa.md).
Pilixa, the old cracked-skin apothecary and healer, tends to a living lavender plant cradled gently in a baby hammock.

## The hunt for Iron Pot

The party sets out through the cinder alleys with a resolute objective: locate and destroy the sixth and final surviving hull of [Iron Pot 6-body](../../directory/LastSerai/IronPot6-body.md).
Five of his shells have already been battered or ruined.
If his final body is eliminated, the prince's distributed consciousness will collapse entirely before he can identify his assailants.
Yet despite a week of diligent searching and probing the lower shacks, their quarry remains maddeningly hidden.

## The porcelain storm

Their investigation is halted by a violent storm that strips away all the loose sand around the Last Serai.
The gale churns up the ancient porcelain powder buried beneath the bedrock.
Toxic spores and crackling lightning sweep across the dunes, triggering choking allergies and asthma attacks across the camp.

Pilixa is not so fortunate.
Her body, already battered and weakened by the broadcast from the [Ignored Tower](../../directory/LastSerai/IgnoredTower.md) weeks prior ([ep_009](ep_009.md)), suffers a catastrophic collapse that leaves the healer paraplegic.

## The Final Embassy

Carrying the bedridden Pilixa, the caravaneers march to the Final Embassy of the Spectrum Satraps.
If they cannot eliminate the final hull themselves, they decide to convince the Satraps that Iron Pot is actively scheming to steal the [Harmonium](../../directory/LastSerai/Harmonium.md) and deliver it to the [Porcelain Citadel](../../directory/PorcelainCitadel/index.md).

For a hefty fee, the Satraps grant an audience with their resident seer: an unsettling entity consisting of a vast, swollen head suspended in a vat of glowing nutrient oil.
The head consults the beyond, then placidly announces that there is no way the Porcelain Princes could steal the Harmonium, at least not anytime soon.
The group is told not to worry, and the seer casually prophesies that Pilixa will walk again.
Only after stepping out of the sanctum do the travellers learn the local open secret: the great head is notoriously, unfailingly incorrect in every prophecy it utters.

On the way out through the gatehouse, Kur confers with one of the embassy guards.
Kur pays €100—prudently demanding and keeping an official paper receipt—to secure an immediate introduction to a Satrap "foreman", allegedly a person of decisive action.
After the party lays out the imminent threat posed by Iron Pot's surviving body, the foreman calmly promises the embassy will take care of the matter.
The caravaneers are told to depart and return in two weeks.
